Our PLC control panels automate and monitor industrial and utility processes. A PLC, HMI, drives and sensors are integrated in one enclosure, programmed for your process and tested before dispatch.

We work with the PLC platform you prefer, including Siemens, Schneider, Allen Bradley, Delta, Mitsubishi, ABB and Omron. The panel can connect to plant SCADA over Modbus RTU/TCP, Ethernet, Profibus, Profinet or BACnet.
