This panel runs and protects a reverse osmosis water treatment plant. It controls the high-pressure, feed, transfer and dosing pumps from pressure and level signals, and shuts down safely on dry run, overload, phase failure, single phasing, under-voltage or earth fault.

The standard panel uses DOL starters with auto/manual selection. PLC, HMI, VFD and SCADA options are available for larger or fully automated plants.
