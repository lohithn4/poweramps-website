A tap-off box draws power from busbar trunking and feeds it to machines, sub-distribution boards or other loads along the run. It plugs straight onto the bus duct, so loads can be added or moved without new cabling from the main panel.

Each box is fitted with the protection your load needs (MCCB, MCB or fuse-switch units), with metering and interlocks as required.
