A DG synchronization panel lets two or more diesel generators run in parallel as one power source. It matches voltage, frequency and phase sequence before closing each breaker, shares the load between running sets, and starts or stops generators as demand changes.

Protection includes reverse power, overload, short circuit and over-frequency. Black start is available as an option.
