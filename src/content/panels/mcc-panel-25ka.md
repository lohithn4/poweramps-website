A Motor Control Centre brings the starting, protection and monitoring of many motors into a single, organised line-up. Each motor gets its own compartment with an isolator, starter and indication, fed from a common busbar behind a metered incomer.

Our MCC panels are built to order. Feeder count, starter type (DOL, star-delta, soft starter or VFD) and construction (fixed or draw-out) are matched to your motor list and site.
