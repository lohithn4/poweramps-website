The UPS input/output distribution panel sits between the utility supply, the UPS and the critical loads. It handles the UPS input, output and bypass switching, so the UPS can be serviced without dropping the load.

Configurations include ACB or MCCB incomers, MCB or MCCB outgoing feeders, digital metering and optional surge protection.
