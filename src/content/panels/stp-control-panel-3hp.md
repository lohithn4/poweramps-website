Our STP control panel automates a sewage treatment plant end to end. It runs raw-sewage and transfer pumps, air blowers, agitators and dosing pumps on level and timer logic, so the plant runs unattended.

The standard build suits MBBR plants with three motors. Larger plants can have MCC sections, a PLC with HMI, VFDs and SCADA connectivity.
