Our fire pump control panel starts and supervises the main electric pump, diesel pump and jockey pump of a hydrant or sprinkler system. Pumps start automatically on pressure drop, change over if one fails, and can be run manually in an emergency.

Starters can be DOL, star-delta or soft starter, with full motor protection and an interface to the building fire alarm.
