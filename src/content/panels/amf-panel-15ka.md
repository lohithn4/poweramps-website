An AMF (automatic mains failure) panel watches the grid supply. When the mains fails it starts the generator, transfers the load, and switches back when the mains returns.

This panel uses the Micromann GC09 controller, with auto and manual modes, simple AMF logic programming and an EB bypass switch.
